# Analysis Overview

Through these datasets, the analysis helps provide better insights into the **evolution of global communication technologies**.

---

# 1. Growth of Telecommunication Technologies

<img width="1790" height="490" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/472eb93d-578b-476d-8a71-77ce48ab4cba" />

The first insight analyzes the growth of telecommunication technologies such as:

- Telephone
- Mobile Phones
- Broadband Internet

### Global Telephone Usage

The **Global Telephone Usage** graph shows that landline telephones dominated global communication for almost **40 years** due to strong infrastructure expansion.

However, after **2010**, the growth begins to decline due to the introduction and widespread adoption of **mobile phones**.

---

### Global Mobile Phone Usage

The **Global Mobile Phone Usage** graph shows rapid growth starting in the **late 1990s**.

After **2010**, mobile phone usage increases significantly and reaches a global peak around **2015**.

However, after **2022**, mobile subscription growth begins to slow down as **internet-based communication technologies expand**.

---

### Global Broadband Usage

The **Global Broadband Usage** graph shows that broadband subscriptions begin increasing steadily after **2005**.

After **2022**, there is an accelerated expansion in broadband usage. However, its adoption is still moderated by the widespread global use of **mobile communication networks**.

---

# 2. Communication Technology Crossover

The **Communication Technology Crossover Graph** helps identify the time periods when one communication technology surpasses another.

<img width="988" height="545" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/417deb7f-5da0-4876-b337-e9084f97aa46" />

### Key Observations

- The **telephone line (blue)** remains relatively stable for many years.
- The **mobile phone line (orange)** grows exponentially starting in the **late 1990s**.
- In the **early 2000s**, mobile phone usage surpasses telephone usage.

This transition represents the **Leapfrog Effect**, where a newer technology bypasses the limitations of older infrastructure.

---

### Broadband Growth

The **broadband line (green)** begins increasing steadily after **2005**.

Around **2021**, broadband subscriptions surpass telephone subscriptions, reflecting the rise of:

- Video calls
- Streaming communication
- Internet messaging platforms

After **2022**, mobile communication shows a slight decline due to the rapid growth of **Zoom, VoIP platforms, and other internet communication tools**, especially following the **COVID-19 pandemic**.

---

# 3. Internet Communication Adoption

Future communication trends are increasingly shifting toward **internet-based communication**.

The **Internet Communication Adoption Curve** provides insight into how telecommunications may evolve in the future.

<img width="859" height="545" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d33465f4-187f-4cd7-8915-6a8a997731f2" />

### Key Observations

- Major changes begin after **2000**, driven by expanding internet infrastructure.
- The most significant increase occurs after **2020**, largely due to global digital transformation during the **COVID-19 pandemic**.

The adoption curve is **partially S-shaped**, which reflects the typical pattern of technological adoption:

1. Introduction of new technology  
2. Rapid growth and adoption  
3. Maturity and integration into society

---

# Conclusion

The analysis highlights the transformation of communication technologies through three major stages:

```
Telephone → Mobile Phones → Internet Communication
```

This evolution demonstrates how technological innovation continuously reshapes global communication systems.

With the ongoing expansion of internet infrastructure, **internet-based communication is expected to dominate future telecommunications.**
